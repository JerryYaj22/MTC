Items must be returned in their original condition. Opened, used, or damaged products may not be eligible for return.

Due to the collectible nature of trading cards, opened products may have additional return restrictions. Please contact us before returning card products.

If your order arrives damaged or you receive the wrong item, please contact us as soon as possible with photos of the item and packaging.
