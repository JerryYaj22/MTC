Returns & Exchanges

We want you to be happy with your purchase. If there is an issue with your order, please contact us and we will be happy to assist you.

Return Requests

Return requests should be submitted within 30 days of receiving your order. Customers are responsible for return shipping costs when requesting a refund for items that are not damaged, defective, or incorrect.

Damaged Item Claims

Return requests for damaged item claims should be submitted within 7 days of receiving your order. Customers are to document any shipping damage upon opening their package. Claims for damaged items must include video recording of the unopened package, the packaging materials, and the damaged item. Failure to provide adequate documentation may result in the claim being denied.

Trading Card & TCG Final Sale Items

All trading card products, including single cards, booster packs, booster boxes, and other TCG items, are final sale and are not eligible for returns or refunds unless they arrive damaged or we shipped the wrong item.
